## Featured Project

### [Argus](https://github.com/SjM1NzNS/Argus)

An authorization-first security research framework combining:

- an Obsidian knowledge base;
- reusable Hermes Agent skills;
- Web2 and Web3 vulnerability playbooks;
- source-first research workflows;
- evidence and false-positive review gates; and
- deterministic tooling for learning, validation, and public-release safety.

Argus is designed as a research command center—not a push-button vulnerability scanner. It helps turn reconnaissance, hypotheses, testing, evidence, and reporting into a repeatable workflow.

## Research Principles

- Authorization and scope before testing
- Evidence before conclusions
- Reproducibility over speculation
- Minimal and non-destructive validation
- Clear separation of public knowledge and sensitive research data

> Repositories are published when they are useful, documented, reproducible, and safe to share.
